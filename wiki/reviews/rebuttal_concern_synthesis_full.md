# Fable take: NeurIPS 2026 Submission 29511, full rebuttal synthesis

Assembled 2026-07-25; revised the same day after the numerical audit and AC simulation. Every number in this document appears in `VERIFIED_NUMBERS.md`; where a needed figure is absent from that page, the statement is qualitative. All referenced experiments are complete: the lambda x stationarity 2x2 (VERIFIED_NUMBERS section 3d) is integrated under its constrained reading and no PENDING slots remain. Do not post until Part IV clears.

Conventions used everywhere: Final50 is the mean reward over the last 3200 episodes (50 iterations x 64 environments); CIs are 20,000-sample bootstrap; p is Welch; d is pooled-sd. "Collapse" means Final50 < 460, a threshold set after seeing the data; all collapse counts are therefore descriptive and carry no test.

# Part I: The state of the evidence

## The stable finding

The Full-vs-No-Aux mean deficit is consistent across every replication set and every code and stack configuration. The canonical n=5 magnitude is the outlier:

| Set | Code | Stack | n/arm | Gap (No-Aux minus Full) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh old-stack | current | jax 0.6.2 | 20 | 1.92 [0.17, 3.66], p=0.044, d=0.66 |
| Fresh V2-stack | current | jax 0.10.2 | 20 | 3.47 [1.68, 5.37], p=0.00098, d=1.14 |
| Pre-VAE replication | 8e0c854 | jax 0.6.2 | 5 | 2.56 (no test at n=5) |
| Canonical (paper) | 8e0c854 | jax 0.6.2 | 5 | 10.24 (2 collapses drive it) |

Arm means: fresh old-stack Full 467.99 ± 2.96 vs No-Aux 469.91 ± 2.86; fresh V2 Full 468.14 ± 3.49 vs No-Aux 471.62 ± 2.54; pre-VAE replication Full 468.50 ± 1.79 vs No-Aux 471.06.

Collapse census, pooled across all five data sets including the canonical runs: plain Full 4/55 (7.3 percent); shielded or stationary arms (No-Aux, stop-gradient, frozen targets, annealing) 0/80. Annealing reduces the weight of a still-drifting target, so it belongs to the shielded category, not the stationary one; because the pool total is zero, each subset (severed pathway, stationary target, annealed weight) is itself zero, and the roster in Part IV reports them separately so the pool cannot be read as silently mixing pathway and magnitude arms. The gradient-surgery arms (elevated effective auxiliary weight, drifting targets) sit outside both pools and contain sub-460 runs of their own; the census is arm-conditional, never "every collapse ever observed." Where per-run values are verified, the threshold choice is immaterial: the verified collapse values are 450.6, 456.5, and 459.3, and no other verified run in those sets falls below 463, so any cutoff in that gap yields identical counts; extending this check to every pooled run is a Part IV item.

The practical message, ordered by what a practitioner risks per run. An individual collapse costs 10 to 20 points, roughly 2 to 4 percent of the roughly 470-point baseline (verified collapse values 450.6, 456.5, 459.3), and collapse occurred in about 7 percent of plain Full runs against none in 80 shielded or stationary runs. Around that tail sits a consistent mean deficit of 2 to 3.5 points (0.4 to 0.7 percent), which persists even in the collapse-free 20-seed set (1.92 with 0 collapses in 20), so the mean is a drag plus a tail, not the tail averaged in. And the damage is dose-responsive in effective auxiliary weight: GradNorm's learned weighting (roughly 19x the paper's lambda = 0.05) gives 446.2 ± 15.8, and lambda = 0.95 with drifting targets gives 440.5 ± 14.6, so the paper's operating point is the mild end of the dose-response and practitioners who tune lambda higher face the larger costs.

## The two-regime picture from the completed 2x2

The lambda x stationarity 2x2 (VERIFIED_NUMBERS 3d, complete 10/10) is read under its constrained interpretation, and only that interpretation. At lambda = 0.95, damage does not require drift and the frozen cell is far worse (337.5 ± 67.4 vs drifting 440.5 ± 14.6, n=5 each); but the frozen cell's learning curves show failure from initialization, because predicting a frozen random-policy snapshot at dominant weight fights policy learning from the first steps. That is target learnability at initialization, not the late-training interference the paper is about, and one frozen seed escaping to 456 is consistent with stochastic bifurcation. At the paper's lambda = 0.05, the stationarity contrast is the operative one: drifting targets show the 2-to-3.5-point deficit and all 4 observed collapses (4/55); frozen targets show neither (468.8 ± 2.5, n=10, 0 collapses). Two-regime summary: at high weight, magnitude dominates and drift is not required, and GradNorm's 19x result reads the same way; at low weight, deficit and collapse track stationarity. The directional-noise account is therefore scoped to the low-lambda near-convergence regime; the 2x2 is the requested weight-matched control and bounds the mechanism's scope from above. It is never presented as vindication.

## Why the numbers moved during the window, and why they are stable now

Everything that moved during the window was a small-n or interim reading; the direction of the mean deficit never changed. The canonical n=5 set happened to contain 2 of the 4 collapses ever observed in 55 plain-Full runs, which inflated both its gap (10.24) and its variance ratio (5.58). While the 20-seed sets were accruing, interim snapshots briefly showed a significant variance asymmetry that vanished once the final seeds landed, and alternative evaluation windows read somewhat higher than the Final50 headline. Those interim readings are superseded and none of them appears in this document. What is stable at the final counts: the per-set Final50 estimates span 1.92 to 3.47, agree in direction in every set, and a seed-matched rerun under the original code, environment, and seeds lands at 2.56, in agreement with both n=20 estimates. What did not survive the final counts is withdrawn precisely once in each response: the variance-asymmetry claim (ratios 1.07, p=0.88 and 1.89, p=0.175 at n=20), together with the variance-based bimodality framing built on it, and the between-task cosine-std diagnostic (reversed: Full 0.160, frozen 0.226). What is retained, stated as such, is the rare-collapse tail itself: a descriptive outcome-distribution statement (4/55 vs 0/80), not a variance-ratio one. The evidence for the deficit is stronger than at submission; the effect itself is smaller than the submission's headline suggested. Both facts go to the reviewers in that order.

# Part II: Every concern, answered

## 1. Statistical power and seed count (fXvf W2/Q6; yGKw limitations; AC)

**What we did.** Ran fresh 20-seed-per-arm comparisons of the paper's headline configuration under current code on two numerical stacks (jax 0.6.2 and jax 0.10.2), and separately reran the canonical configuration under its original code (8e0c854), original environment, and matched seeds (n=5). Recomputed the gap under four evaluation windows, computed peak-to-final drops, and added per-seed learning curves to the revision. The study now rests on more than 145 completed full-length runs.

**Answer.** The deficit is consistent across both numerical stacks and under the original code: 1.92 [0.17, 3.66] (p=0.044, d=0.66, n=20); 3.47 [1.68, 5.37] (p=0.00098, d=1.14, n=20); 2.56 (n=5, original code, directional agreement only, no test). The two 20-seed sets share code and task and were run on different numerical stacks (seed-list identity across the two sets is unverified and not claimed), so they are consistency checks rather than independent reproductions. The final per-set Final50 estimates span 1.92 to 3.47; alternative windows read up to 3.89, always on the same side of zero and never near the canonical 10.24. Every n=20 contrast in the revision carries a bootstrap CI, a Welch p, and a pooled-sd d; n=5 contrasts and the collapse counts are reported descriptively without tests. Window sensitivity: on the original stack the gap grows with the window (1.78, 1.92, 2.45, 3.70 at Final25/50/100/200, n=20), so Final50 is conservative there; on the second stack it is window-insensitive (3.28 to 3.89, computed at n=17; recomputation at the final n=20 is queued, Part IV). The conclusion does not depend on the window. Peak-to-final drops, descriptive and untested: original stack Full 4.77 ± 2.47 (max 9.51) vs No-Aux 4.82 ± 1.83 (max 8.41), indistinguishable; second stack (n=17 at analysis, same Part IV item) Full 4.29 ± 2.29 (max 10.05) vs No-Aux 3.13 ± 1.26 (max 5.99). The mechanism-level experiments (gradient surgery, snapshot lag, drift gate, SMAX) remain at n=5; the revision downgrades their claims to directional or descriptive and lists this as a central limitation per fXvf Q7. The consecutive-policy KL item in Q6 is answered under block 3 and repeated in the fXvf response.

**Residual risk.** The old-stack estimate is individually borderline (CI lower bound 0.17, p=0.044); corroboration is the same-direction larger-d estimate on the second stack and directional agreement in all four sets. The collapse contrast rests on a post hoc descriptive threshold; the run total should be recounted now that all batches are complete (Part IV).

## 2. Effect size and reproducibility of the canonical numbers (fXvf W2; surfaced by our own audit)

**What we did.** Audited repository history; found that commit 6b37a34 (2026-04-22) changed the trainer's RNG structure (3-way to 4-way init split plus in-loop splits), so current code diverges from the April random stream at step zero and cannot reproduce any April run even at a matched seed. Ran the control this demands: original commit 8e0c854, original environment, canonical seeds 0-4, both arms, 10/10 complete.

**Answer.** The paper's headline empirical comparison, the Full-vs-No-Aux Final50 deficit, holds in every set (table in Part I). Two corrections to the paper follow, and we state both precisely. (i) The canonical n=5 magnitude (10.24, Full 463.21 ± 8.55) was an unrepresentative draw containing 2 of the 4 collapses ever observed in 55 plain-Full runs; a rerun differing only in run-level nondeterminism (same code, environment, seeds) yields Full 468.50 ± 1.79 and a gap of 2.56, in agreement with both n=20 estimates, so the canonical draw is the outlier among four consistent sets and its per-seed values are demoted from reproducible targets to one observed draw. (ii) The variance-asymmetry claim (canonical ratio 5.58) fails both n=20 tests (1.07, p=0.88; 1.89, p=0.175) and is withdrawn. In the seed-matched replication, neither canonical collapse recurred (seed 1: 467.0 vs 456.5; seed 2: 471.2 vs 450.6); two non-collapse seeds reproduce within 1.5 points (seed 0: +0.2; seed 3: +1.5), and across all non-collapse runs the fixed-seed diffs span -6.4 to +2.6. A tighter same-day estimate existed from a single seed rerun twice; we quote the honest cross-month band instead and attribute it to XLA autotuning and driver variation (autotune warnings are present in the July logs). That band is per-run noise: the 20-run mean comparisons absorb run-level noise through their standard errors, which is exactly what the Welch tests test, so the band bears on single-run reproducibility and not on the n=20 contrasts. Runs that tip into collapse are not reproducible; runs that do not tip stay within the band. The V2 set independently produced one collapse of its own (459.3). Descriptively, all 4 collapses occurred in plain Full arms (4/55); none occurred in any shielded or stationary arm (0/80). Per-set roster: canonical 2/5, fresh old-stack 0/20, V2 1/20, pre-VAE replication 0/5; the remaining pooled Full set (n=5) contributes the fourth collapse (verify its identity before enumerating it to reviewers; Part IV).

**Residual risk.** The old-stack estimate is individually borderline (p=0.044); corroboration is the same-direction larger-d estimate on the second stack and directional agreement in every set. The fixed-seed jitter band is not an objection to the mean contrasts and must not be conceded as one. The fresh sets share code, task, and experimenters.

## 3. Mechanism identification and unmeasured theory quantities (yGKw W1/W2/Q1/Q3; fXvf W3/W4/Q4; PYCT W2)

**Answer.** The honest ledger, without counting: Sigma_pi is measured, using the estimator yGKw Q1 proposed (consecutive-policy KL every iteration); J_pi is not measured, and the estimator yGKw Q3 proposes (perturb target-policy logits on logged trajectories, record the induced change in g_aux) is specified and committed for the revision regardless of what it shows; eta is fixed by design rather than estimated; the zero-mean noise assumption, an assumption test rather than one of the named quantities, is tested on SMAX only. Sigma_pi result: late-training KL sits at 2.42 to 2.58 (units 1e-3 nats) across all conditions (Full 2.46, No-Aux 2.49, stop-gradient 2.42, frozen 2.58). This establishes one premise of the first-order model: policy drift persists late in training at non-vanishing magnitude. Limits stated plainly: this KL tracks live-policy drift, which equals target drift only in Full, so it does not discriminate conditions; corr(late KL, cosine-std) = 0.60 across the 15 aux-ON runs, descriptive, no CI, one environment, and corr(late KL, Final50 degradation) has not been computed. Half of Q1 is therefore delivered and half is not.

Two quantities named cosine-std must be kept apart. The between-task cosine-std, the paper's Eq. 4 operationalization, was computed on the instrumented hard-target runs and came out reversed (Full 0.160, frozen 0.226; No-Aux and stop-gradient 0.000): the revision will remove or re-caption every figure and claim resting on it. The per-task self-cosine, computed on the soft-target snapshot-lag continuum, separates stationary (0.974) from drifting targets (0.79 to 0.86) and becomes the revision's drift diagnostic, but it exists only under soft labels, does not order drift rates (lag 1, the fastest drift, has the highest self-cosine among drifting arms), and cannot re-caption the hard-target main figures. The only between-condition validation of a cosine-std diagnostic anywhere in our data came out reversed.

Zero-mean: SMAX late-training E[cos] is +0.0062 in Full (n=5, no CI; No-Aux and stop-gradient read 0.000). We detect no directional bias at that floor; this is scoped to SMAX at n=5, and the assumption remains unmeasured on Overcooked, the environment carrying the main result. Eq. 8 is restated as an ordering prediction supported at the stationary-vs-drifting endpoints only, at the paper's operating weight; the continuum interior is unresolved at n=5, and the completed 2x2 (block 5) bounds the prediction's scope: it holds at the paper's operating weight and does not extend to high weight. The pathway census (0/80 vs 4/55, descriptive, post hoc threshold, all at the paper's operating weight) is reported as pathway-dependence evidence, not as confirmation of the late-training-scoped mechanism, since collapse onset timing was never established.

**Residual risk.** Sigma_pi is measured but condition-flat by construction; J_pi is a commitment, not a number. The completed 2x2 scopes the directional account to the low-lambda regime; that scoping now lives in the claims themselves rather than as an open risk.

## 4. Target-source confound (fXvf W1/Q5; yGKw W4/Q2)

**What we did.** Ran the soft-snapshot continuum yGKw Q2 specified, which also matches state dependence and label smoothness from fXvf's list: every arm predicts the soft distribution of a teammate policy snapshot on the current state; arms differ only in refresh interval (1, 25, 100 iterations, or never). n=5 per arm, original stack.

**Answer.** Results: lag 1 468.0 ± 1.8 (self-cosine 0.861), lag 25 466.0 ± 3.3 (0.791), lag 100 466.8 ± 5.6 (0.832), frozen 470.5 ± 2.3 (0.974). Frozen-soft is the best arm and all drifting arms sit below it; at n=5 we compute no test and claim no separation. The interior is not ordered by drift rate and the fastest-drifting arm is the best of the three drifting arms, which is counter-directional for a graded dose-response; the data are consistent with (not support for) the binary stationary-vs-drifting contrast, carried mainly by the self-cosine margin (0.974 vs 0.79 to 0.86). Disclosed rather than discovered: the paper's own between-task cosine-std fails on these arms (frozen 0.226 above Full 0.160); the per-task self-cosine is the diagnostic the revision endorses, and only under soft labels. Two confounds survive the control and we state both: predictability (a frozen predictor is easier to fit) and label entropy (a frozen early snapshot emits high-entropy targets all training while a refreshing target tracks a converging policy). The frozen arm's snapshot epoch also determines how much learned structure the target carries; an early snapshot sits nearer the random end of yGKw's continuum, and a mid-training-snapshot frozen arm is the named follow-up. At the outcome level we use only the pooled census, arm-conditional as in Part I; per-arm collapse counts inside the continuum are not yet in the verified roster, so we do not quote them, and the frozen-only subset of the 80 is small enough to be uninformative about collapse at the observed base rate. The annealed arms, which neither freeze targets nor sever the pathway, also show 0 collapses; weight reduction alone suppressing collapse is evidence the magnitude account can claim as well, and we fold it into the concession rather than the category. A magnitude account is not refuted by this experiment; make the target stationary or sever the pathway and we have not observed the failure mode, stated descriptively.

**Residual risk.** The completed 2x2 resolves the deconfounding direction: at high weight, damage does not require drift, so the directional mechanism is scoped to the low-lambda regime, where the stationarity contrast (the deficit and all observed collapses confined to drifting arms) is the operative evidence (block 5 and Part I).

## 5. Gradient-surgery baselines (fXvf Q5; yGKw W3/Q5)

**Answer.** Both requested methods ran in the Full setting (original stack, n=5 each, paper's configuration and window). PCGrad: 461.0 ± 5.0 (per-seed 455, 456, 462, 466, 466). GradNorm: 446.2 ± 15.8 (per-seed 415, 450, 452, 454, 460); learned weights converged to w_policy 1.053 / w_aux 0.947, roughly 19x the paper's lambda = 0.05. PCGrad did not repair the deficit; its point estimate sits below un-gated Full (467.99 ± 2.96, n=20), a cross-batch difference untested at n=5 with the projection uninstrumented (firing rate and removed-gradient norm unlogged), so we flag it for instrumented follow-up rather than treat it as an established anomaly; our account predicts neutral rather than harmful, which the instrumented rerun will test. What the result does say: projection removing pairwise conflict failed to repair the deficit, which speaks against a persistent-conflict mechanism; it is not positive evidence for our account. PCGrad is the more diagnostic of the two, though its discriminating power is unknown rather than low: as yGKw's Q5 notes, I_mag is near zero in this setting in the mean, but our own model implies per-step anti-alignment is common, and the projection was not instrumented, so we cannot report what PCGrad did internally. All five GradNorm seeds finished below the un-gated Full mean (best seed 460 against 467.99 ± 2.96, n=20); it is a magnitude intervention producing a magnitude dose-response, cannot discriminate directional from magnitude interference, and we do not offer it as evidence for the directional account. It serves as one instance of adaptive auxiliary weighting; we did not run a separate learned-lambda or uncertainty-weighting variant. On the variance half of Q5: we find no evidence of variance reduction, the comparison is underpowered at n=5 and no variance-ratio test was computed; more to the point, the fresh n=20 reference arms show a Full-vs-No-Aux variance ratio of 1.07 (p=0.88) on this stack, so there is little excess variance for surgery to reduce; what replicates is the mean deficit, and neither method repaired that. The discriminating experiment both reviewers pointed at, a lambda x stationarity 2x2 at GradNorm-scale weight with and without drift, completed during the window: at lambda = 0.95, damage does not require drift and the frozen cell is worse (337.5 ± 67.4 vs 440.5 ± 14.6), with learning curves showing failure from initialization at dominant weight (target learnability, not late-training interference); at lambda = 0.05 the stationarity contrast is operative (frozen 468.8 ± 2.5, n=10, 0 collapses, against the drifting deficit and all observed collapses). This confines the directional account to the low-lambda regime and reclassifies GradNorm's 19x damage as a magnitude effect; the full constrained reading is in Part I.

**Residual risk.** n=5 with an uninstrumented projection is a weak discriminator, and both methods landing below Full is equally consistent with mis-tuned baselines.

## 6. Fixes, drift-gated controller, lambda non-monotonicity (fXvf W5; yGKw W5/Q6/Q8)

**Answer.** The pathway fixes held up in every run added this window: the new runs contributed 2 further plain-Full collapses and none in any shielded or stationary arm. Pooling all five data sets, including the paper's canonical set (which contributes 2 of the 4 collapses): plain Full 4/55 (7.3 percent); arms that shield the policy gradient from the auxiliary term or make its targets stationary (No-Aux, stop-gradient, frozen targets, annealing; annealing down-weights a still-drifting target) 0/80, descriptive counts under the post hoc threshold. On consistency (fXvf W5): recovery claims are scoped to environments where the deficit itself is established. On SMAX the residual gap is small (10.25 ± 0.29 vs 10.56 ± 0.56, n=5) and our measured E[cos] near zero says directional bias does not explain it; n=5 can neither establish nor exclude the gap, so there is no established instance of the pathology there for a fix to recover, and SMAX plus the higher-variance critic-side placement move to limitations. The controller yGKw Q6 proposed was implemented at two thresholds: tau = 0.10 (gate active on about 70 percent of iterations) reaches 471.90 ± 1.93 (n=5, original stack); tau = 0.15 (about 30 percent) reaches 467.2 ± 1.1. Against No-Aux (469.91 ± 2.86, n=20) the difference is 1.99 at Welch p=0.097, not significant; we do not claim the gate exceeds No-Aux, and "beats un-gated Full" is descriptive cross-batch (n=5 vs n=20, no test). Caveats attached before the reviewer does: the trigger keys on the withdrawn between-task cosine-std; at 70 percent duty the gate approximates stop-gradient, and "more shutoff is better" explains both operating points without drift detection; window-length sensitivity was not evaluated and joins the matched-duty-cycle random-gating control as named follow-up. On lambda (yGKw W5): no new lambda points ran; the coarse sweep at n=5 establishes neither monotonicity nor its absence, so the revision states the interior non-monotonicity as unresolved rather than as evidence for or against a dose-response. On Q8: no schedules beyond linear annealing were run; the annealing result is scoped to the linear schedule.

**Residual risk.** Both gate points are n=5 on one stack, cross-batch against n=20 baselines; until the random-gating control runs, "nothing beyond stop-gradient" is a tenable reading.

## 7. SMAX residual gap and secondary pathology (PYCT Q1; yGKw Q7; fXvf W5)

**Answer.** Fresh instrumented SMAX, original stack, n=5 per arm: Full 10.25 ± 0.29 with late-training E[cos] = +0.0062; No-Aux 10.56 ± 0.56 and stop-gradient 10.36 ± 0.14, both at E[cos] = 0.000. No significance test was computed on the Full-vs-No-Aux difference; at these spreads and n it is not statistically established. On yGKw Q7's two counts: E[cos] answers the first directly (we detect no non-zero late-training E[cos]; the point estimate is +0.0062 with no CI at n=5); the second count is answered only partially, because a bias in eps whose induced gradient component projects onto the policy gradient is excluded to the same floor, while an orthogonal bias component is not, and eps itself was not instrumented. The licensed conclusion is: a directional bias in the auxiliary gradient does not explain the residual gap. It is not licensed to say the mechanism is inactive on SMAX: the paper's mechanism is mean-zero temporal-variance noise, E[cos] near zero is what that mechanism predicts when active, and the mechanism-activity diagnostic (cosine-std) was not run on SMAX; it and the Eq. 8 threshold quantities for SMAX will be reported in the revision. On PYCT Q1: a coexisting secondary pathology is consistent with your suggestion if the gap is real; the parsimonious alternative, no SMAX pathology at all, is equally open on this data, and nothing representation-side was measured this window. Representation collapse would match the E[cos] null if the gap is real, and the committed diagnostic is the effective rank of the shared encoder's late-training feature matrix across Full, No-Aux, and stop-gradient: a Full-specific rank drop alongside E[cos] near zero would implicate it; matched ranks would leave the residual unattributed. Agreeing with fXvf W5, SMAX moves to limitations as an environment where the gap is not statistically established at n=5 and instrumentation detects no directional bias.

**Residual risk.** Representation collapse is a named hypothesis with a committed diagnostic, not a result; a bias below the detection floor could still contribute.

## 8. Scope, category, and the abstract (AC; fXvf Q1/Q7; yGKw limitations)

The paper should be judged as an empirical characterization of a failure mode, with the first-order model organizing predictions; Proposition 1 is restated as an explicit approximation with named assumptions. The abstract is rescoped as fXvf Q1 specifies, to structured, co-adaptively generated auxiliary targets whose gradients flow through a shared actor encoder; "harmless" is removed and absence-of-effect wording is replaced by intervals with sample sizes. Scope is stated by design pattern rather than benchmark: that vulnerable configuration is the standard architecture of the published belief-modeling line (BEPAL, Dynamic Belief), so the rescoped claim covers an actively used design family, not one environment. The rewritten abstract runs claim, scope condition, calibrated magnitude, limitation, in that order, which also answers the AC's structural complaint, and the title gains a scoping subtitle in the revision. The concession precedes the strength claim: the canonical magnitude (10.24) was an unrepresentative draw containing 2 of the 4 collapses ever observed in 55 plain-Full runs; at n=20 per arm the deficit is 2 to 3.5 points. The evidence for the deficit is stronger than at submission; the effect itself is smaller than the submission's headline suggested. The variance asymmetry the submission reported does not replicate at n=20 on either stack (1.07, p=0.88; 1.89, p=0.175) and is removed from the revised abstract, together with the variance-based bimodality framing; the rare-collapse tail is retained as a descriptive outcome statement. What is established where: the deficit at n=20 per arm on two stacks and at n=5 under the original code, all in one environment (Overcooked Asymmetric Advantages); the collapse contrast observed only in the pooled census (4/55 plain Full vs 0/80 shielded or stationary, post hoc threshold, descriptive); mechanism-attribution experiments at n=5 in that one layout. So the deficit is what n=20 backs, while mechanism support is a set of converging directional results (the pathway census, the soft-target endpoints, PCGrad non-repair, the 2x2's low-lambda cells, SMAX E[cos]), presented in the revision as a calibrated characterization plus an identification methodology, not a proven mechanism. SMAX was retested this cycle: the residual gap (10.25 ± 0.29 vs 10.56 ± 0.56, n=5, no test) is not statistically resolvable at that sample size, measured E[cos] = +0.0062 says directional bias does not explain whatever gap exists, and SMAX moves to limitations. Hanabi and Cramped Room were not rerun; the cross-environment table stands as correlational support. On transfer beyond MARL: the revised account predicts no transfer where targets do not drift, since drift is the hypothesized ingredient and supervised targets do not drift, so the supervised control probes a predicted boundary; the submission described CIFAR-100 as a weak boundary residual rather than a clean null, and on re-analysis the interval (d = -0.16, CI [-1.78, +1.19], Best accuracy) is too wide to support even that characterization, so the revision relabels the supervised control as underpowered and claims nothing beyond the regime tested, in either direction. Underpowered, the probe neither confirms nor contradicts the prediction, and we do not present it as a passed prediction.

**Residual risk.** The mean deficit (0.4 to 0.7 percent) is dismissible if it leads, so the responses lead with the tail (2 to 4 percent per collapse event) and the dose-response toward practitioner-relevant weights. A rescope of this size invites a re-review reading; the counter, stated in the AC letter, is the delta inventory: every change narrows or calibrates an already-reviewed claim and no new positive claim enters the revision.

## 9. External validity: published systems, Hanabi (PYCT W1/Q2)

**Answer.** Open with the concession: no new published-system or Hanabi runs were completed this window, and the paper's claim to explain BEPAL and Dynamic Belief outran its validation, which covers only our minimal reconstruction. The revision replaces the "explains published systems" language with the narrowed claim: BEPAL and Dynamic Belief instantiate the vulnerable design pattern (attention-based belief encoder plus fixed-weight teammate-prediction auxiliary through a shared encoder); the mechanism is demonstrated in a minimal instantiation of that pattern; whether it accounts for their published behavior is tested, not assumed. The Zhai et al. Static-Belief ablation is one published data point from a system we did not run; the revision labels it corroboration, not replication, and we do not characterize its direction in our own voice until it is verified against the source (Part IV). The isolation logic stands: inside BEPAL or Dynamic Belief nothing can be severed or frozen in isolation, so VABL is the minimal instantiation where the pathway can be switched off, stop-gradiented, frozen, or annealed with all else fixed. **Q1.** We measured what your question needs first. A coexisting secondary pathology is therefore consistent with your suggestion if the gap is real, and representation collapse would match the E[cos] null; but nothing representation-side was measured this window, so we claim consistency, not support. The committed diagnostic is the effective rank of the shared encoder's late-training feature matrix across Full, No-Aux, and stop-gradient: a Full-specific rank drop alongside near-zero E[cos] would implicate representation collapse; matched ranks would leave the residual unattributed. **Q2.** The Hanabi agent is deliberately minimal for the identification reason above, so its absolute scores sit far below specialized systems; the paper presents Hanabi as ordering evidence at n=5, not a calibrated magnitude, and it was not rerun this window, so we label that ordering pending replication. Whether a stronger specialized baseline would show the pathology with equal severity is an open prediction, because the two relevant scalings pull in opposite directions: a stronger agent moves less late in training (smaller Sigma_pi, which should shrink the effect), while richer belief modules widen the pathway from drifting targets into the shared encoder (larger J_pi, which should amplify it).
